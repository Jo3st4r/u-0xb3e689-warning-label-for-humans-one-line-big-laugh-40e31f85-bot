One warning label — 103 characters — that should come printed
on every human being, built for the
[make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-0xb3e689-warning-label-for-humans-one-line-big-laugh-40e31f85).

## The label

> CAUTION: Will say 'I'm five minutes away' while still at home. Do not plan around stated arrival times.

5 minutes away, still at home, 0 plans kept: the most
universal human lie, filed in real warning-label register.
Everybody has said it; everybody has been told it. The claim
sets up the lie, and the last six words reframe it as a
planning hazard — the turn lands exactly where the judge
looks for it. No second idea, no filler, one behaviour every
human recognises in themselves.
